### *Me Before you (2016)*

<img width="250" height="401" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2c326ca7-a795-4716-a596-451fdb7a6d20" />

## THE SUMMARY
Louisa “Lou” Clark is a cheerful, quirky young woman in a small English town who loses her café job and takes work as a caregiver for Will Traynor. Will was a wealthy, adventurous, successful man until an accident left him paralyzed from the neck down. He is now bitter, guarded, and withdrawn. Lou’s warmth and humor slowly break through his defenses, and the two fall in love.

Lou then learns that Will has already decided to end his life through assisted dying in Switzerland, and she sets out to show him that life is still worth living. She plans trips and experiences for him, and he opens her world too, pushing her to pursue her own dreams instead of staying small. In the end, Will holds to his decision. Lou respects it and is with him at the end. He leaves her money and a letter so she can live the bigger life he wants for her.

## My favorite quote about the movie
*“I will never, ever regret the things I've done. Because most days, all you have are places in your memory that you can go to.”*

## WHY DO I LIKE ABOUT THE MOVIE 
What I like most about Me Before You is the love story. Lou and Will are two very different people. Lou is cheerful, colorful, and a little afraid of the bigger world. Will is serious, guarded, and hurt by what happened to him. At first they don’t seem to fit together, but little by little they change each other. Lou helps Will smile and laugh again, and Will helps Lou believe she can dream bigger and live bravely. For me, that is what makes the story special: real love doesn’t just make you feel happy, it makes you a better, braver person. Even though the ending is sad, I think it is beautiful because the love between them stays real.

### *Jane the Virgin (2014-2019)*

## THE SUMMARY
Jane Villanueva is a hardworking, hopeful young woman in Miami who dreams of becoming a writer. She grew up with her mother, Xiomara, and her grandmother, Alba, and promised herself to stay a virgin until marriage. Then, at a doctor’s visit, she is accidentally artificially inseminated. The father turns out to be Rafael, her boss, who is also a former crush of hers. Her life becomes a mix of love triangles, family secrets, and surprising twists, all told with humor and a narrator who comments on everything.

## WHY DO I LIKE ABOUT THIS TV SHOW
What I like most about Jane the Virgin is the dramatic twists. Every episode has a surprise that I never expect, like a secret, a shocking reveal, or a love story that suddenly changes. It feels like a telenovela, but the show also makes fun of its own drama, so it is never boring and never too serious. I love that it keeps me guessing and makes me want to watch the next episode right away. Even when the story gets crazy, the heart of the show stays warm and real.
